### Description

The previous |~ "(?i)(debug|trace)" regex dropped all Alloy logs
that contain trace_id= in their structured fields, resulting in
empty log panels for base_alloy, base_loki, etc. in Grafana.

New regex |~ "(?i)level=(debug|trace)" matches only the log level
field, not any random occurrence of 'trace' in the log line.
