### Description

Adds a discovery.relabel "containers_meta" stage between
discovery.docker "containers" and loki.source.docker "docker_logs"
to extract container_name from __meta_docker_container_name.

This ensures base_*, boot_*, maintenance_* containers get proper
container_name labels in Loki (used by Grafana dashboards like
alertmanager, grafana, prometheus, etc.).

Also removes the redundant local_manager + local_manager_filter +
local_manager_logs blocks (unix socket approach was silently failing
while docker_logs via socket proxy already covered base_* containers).
