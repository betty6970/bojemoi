### Description

IPv4 CIDRs with prefix < /24 are now split into /24 subnets and scanned
until exhaustion. IPv6 CIDRs with prefix < /112 are split into /112
subnets (capped at 256 to avoid infinite iteration on /32→/112 = 2^80).

Each subnet is scanned individually and the XML is deleted after import,
preventing the disk exhaustion caused by multi-GB nmap output files.
