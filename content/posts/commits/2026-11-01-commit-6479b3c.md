### Description

4 functions (store_finding_embedding, find_similar_findings,
store_campaign_summary, find_similar_campaigns) had conn.close() inside
try: — if the query raised an exception, the except block swallowed the
error and conn was never closed, leaking a connection to PG.

Pattern: conn=None before try, finally: if conn: conn.close()
